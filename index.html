Mindful Movement, Serene Spaces

Vani Yoga Center is dedicated to fostering physical wellness, mental resilience, and spiritual harmony. We offer a safe and welcoming space for individuals of all backgrounds, fitness levels, and age groups.

🧘 Beginner-Friendly: All classes are designed for beginners and seasoned practitioners alike. We recommend starting your yoga journey on a Monday to follow our week's theme seamlessly.
🧘‍♂️ Chief Yoga Trainer: Led by Vani, our Chief Yoga Trainer, who ensures personalized guidance and correct postures for every practitioner.

Our Yoga Services 🌿

Choose from a variety of styles and configurations tailored to fit your life, body, and goals.

🧘

Hatha Yoga

Slow-paced, alignment-focused classes that integrate classical yoga postures with pranayama (breathing exercises) to restore physical balance and reduce stress.

⚡

Ashtanga Yoga

A structured, dynamic flow of postures synchronized with deep breathing. Excellent for building muscular strength, cardiovascular endurance, and physical flexibility.

👧

Kids Yoga

Engaging, playful yoga sessions designed for children. Helps improve concentration, motor skills, posture, and emotional regulation (Mon–Fri evening batches).

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Yoga

Bond and stretch together. Combined sessions that allow parents and kids or couples to cultivate mindfulness and fitness collectively.

🌬️

Pranayama & Breathing

Focused yogic breathing techniques to regulate energy levels, oxygenate the body, reduce anxiety, and sharpen mental focus.

🏃

Weight Loss & Flexibility

Targeted, active flows designed to naturally build core strength, burn calories, stimulate digestive fire, and open tight hips and shoulders.

☀️

Suryanamaskar & Mudras

A structured sequence of dynamic sun salutations paired with healing hand gestures (mudras) to balance bodily elements, improve blood circulation, and boost daily energy.

🌸

Stress Relief & Mindfulness

Gentle, restorative yoga flows and meditation designed to activate the nervous system's relaxation response, lower cortisol levels, and foster present-moment awareness.
